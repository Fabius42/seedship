# Seed Ship

Design concepts for Seed Ship, a game about an intelligent AI ship that cultivates and collects alien life across a galaxy. The player evolves lineages of exotic life forms, uplifts them to sapience, and investigates ruins of extinct civilisations to understand what happened while they were asleep.

## About this repo

- `docs/` - dated design notes (`YYYY-MM-DD-title.md`), each one a snapshot of a
  session's thinking.
- `scratchpad.txt` - a running dump of raw, unsorted ideas not yet worked into a
  proper doc.
- `CLAUDE.md` - instructions for AI assistants working in this repo.
