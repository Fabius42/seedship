- There can be good and bad actions that the ship can take. Maybe call them "blue" and "red" ones to avoid too simplistic moralism? These are all in relation to already pre-existing life (either uplifted by the player, or other independently evolved life forms that they discover).
- Blue actions are helpful or neutral to life. Red actions are basically the gardening equivalent of "pruning" or "rooting out" life. This can be useful to make space in a biosphere for a different lineage that the player wants to introduce. Or they might simply do it for fun, in a sim city style way.
- Red actions get unlocked over time as the player progresses and finds more technology in ruins etc. Some are very low-key / surgical (introducing some sort of virus-equivalent) with a chance of only killing that specific lineage, but also potential side effects. Others are all-out violence (orbital bombardment, gigantic laser strikes), the most powerful ones being terraforming actions that change the whole astro body, with potentially devastating effects on the whole biosphere.
- If they lineage is already advanced enough to attribute these activities (both blue and red) to the player, this influences their opinion of them, and also their overall cultural matrix / value system.
